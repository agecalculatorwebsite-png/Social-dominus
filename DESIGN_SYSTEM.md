# Social Dominus — Design System & Visual Identity

## 1. Brand Tokens & Color Palette
- **Primary Violet**: `#7C5CFF` (Primary actions, brand accents, active states)
- **Secondary Sky**: `#38BDF8` (Highlights, positive performance indicators)
- **Dark Neutral Background**: `#070A13` (Deep midnight background)
- **Dark Surface 1**: `#0F1629` (Cards, drawers)
- **Dark Surface 2**: `#18223D` (Sub-cards, interactive hover states)
- **Light Neutral Background**: `#F8FAFC`
- **Light Surface**: `#FFFFFF`
- **Success Emerald**: `#22C55E`
- **Warning Amber**: `#F59E0B`
- **Destructive Rose**: `#EF4444`

## 2. Typography
- Display & Body: Plus Jakarta Sans (with high-legibility tabular figures for metrics and calendars)
- Monospace: JetBrains Mono for IDs, timestamps, and character counters.

## 3. Responsive Breakpoints
- Small Mobile: 320px–374px
- Standard Mobile: 375px–479px
- Large Mobile: 480px–767px
- Tablet: 768px–1023px
- Laptop: 1024px–1279px
- Desktop: 1280px+

## 4. Accessibility & Touch Targets
- Minimum touch target: 44x44px on primary mobile controls.
- WCAG 2.2 AA compliant contrast on dark and light themes.
- Redundant shape and label indicators alongside color indicators for post statuses.
